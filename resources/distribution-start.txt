ULTIMATEHL 3.7 - BIG AG HUD DISTRIBUTION

Extract the entire folder to a writable location and run START HERE.bat.
Do not move UltimateHL.exe away from its Half-Life, BXT and HUD-presets folders.
Close other Half-Life copies first; the normal ownership/Steam setup is required.

Defaults: Big AG HUD, 1x HUD size, 2x-per-axis scene, Tankfird BXT,
full-resolution borderless/DXGI capture path, load FPS floor 0.

Play - windowed control.bat provides an ordinary 1280x800 window, with BXT.
Use "UltimateHL settings..." in the game menu for artwork, HUD size and quality.
Artwork changes require a restart. HUD size and most other settings apply live.
Original 640 and Compact 320 artwork are still available in the settings list.

ABOUT AND FEATURES.txt explains exactly what was built, how it connects to
Half-Life, what was added, the useful commands, and the remaining limitations.
BXT/Tankfird-features.txt has the fork's own notes; BXT/LICENSE is its license.
SHA256SUMS.txt lists the packaged file hashes; it is not a digital signature.

Clean controls: WASD, E use, R reload, Space jump, Ctrl duck, F6 quicksave,
F7 quickload, console on ` / ~, sensitivity 3. No personal binds/saves are shipped.
Your previous playtest folders/configs/saves have not been altered.
